The Path
We've Traveled

A Grassroots Beginning

What started as a simple observation quickly grew into a collective movement. Our first mission involved small-scale pad distributions, but we soon realized the power of community-driven health advocacy.

Scaling the Impact

By partnering with local schools and leaders, we expanded our reach. Every school visited and every community center engaged represented a step forward in eradicating menstrual stigma and providing tangible support.

A Nationwide Movement

Today, our efforts span across multiple states. While we celebrate our numbers, our true achievement lies in the confidence restored and the barriers broken for thousands of individuals.
