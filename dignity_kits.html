About Dignity Kits

Our Dignity Kits are curated packages of essential menstrual hygiene products designed to offer marginalized communities sustainable and reliable tools. They are the frontline of our efforts to eradicate period poverty.

Each kit contains everything necessary to ensure clean and safe menstruation: reusable and biodegradable pads, specialized soaps, clean undergarments for daily transitions, and educational pamphlets packed with health guidance. With these kits, young girls never have to sacrifice their education to manage their cycles.
