Sustainability

Normalizing
Reusable Products

Breaking the stigma around reusable products is crucial for sustainable period equity.

As we move towards a more sustainable future, our approach to menstrual hygiene must also evolve. Reusable menstrual products are not just an environmental choice; they are a powerful tool in the fight against period poverty.

The Economic Impact

For many young people, the recurring cost of disposable pads and tampons is a significant financial burden. Reusable options, such as cloth pads or menstrual cups, provide a long-term solution that saves money and ensures consistency in hygiene management.

Environmental Responsibility

A single person uses thousands of disposable products in their lifetime, most of which end up in landfills. By normalizing reusables, we can drastically reduce our environmental footprint and promote a circular economy in menstrual health.

Overcoming the Stigma

The biggest hurdle to the adoption of reusable products is often the social stigma and lack of education. Through workshops and open dialogue, we can demonstrate that reusables are safe, hygienic, and practical for everyone.
