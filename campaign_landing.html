Period Poverty

Lack of access to sanitary products, menstrual hygiene education, and sanitation facilities, affecting an estimated 500 million people worldwide. Together, we can change this narrative.

‹
›

Goal 1

Ensuring every girl has access to sanitary products and clean water facilities in their schools and homes.

Goal 2

Providing comprehensive menstrual hygiene education to break taboos and empower young women.

Goal 3

Building sustainable sanitation infrastructure to support menstrual dignity in rural communities.

Our Mission Objectives

Goal 4

Engaging local leaders and communities to foster an environment where menstruation is never a barrier.

Goal 5

Advocating for policy changes that eliminate taxes on period products and ensure equal health rights.

Goal 6

Supporting innovative, sustainable, and eco-friendly menstrual solutions for long-term impact.
