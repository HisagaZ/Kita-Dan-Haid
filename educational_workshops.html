About Our Workshops

Our Educational Workshops dismantle systemic taboos by equipping young minds with thorough, scientifically accurate knowledge. We partner hand-in-hand with civic entities to operate fully immersive courses on bodily autonomy and reproductive health.

These inclusive circles go beyond basic biology; they foster safe environments where participants of all genders can demystify menstrual hygiene, promoting empathy across entire ecosystems and ensuring the normalization of health-focused dialogues.
