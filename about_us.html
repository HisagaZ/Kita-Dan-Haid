Our Impact

What We Do

We tackle period poverty through a multi-dimensional approach that ensures long-term sustainability.

Education & Awareness

We host interactive workshops in rural and urban schools to teach menstrual health, reaching over 5,000 students annually.

Kit Distribution

Our "KDH Hygiene Kits" contain reusable pads, soap, and educational materials, designed to last for up to 3 years.

Community Support

We build clean water stations and private sanitation facilities in community centers to provide safe spaces for girls.

Our Story

How It All Began

Pertubuhan Kita dan Haid was born from a simple observation: menstruation is a natural part of life, yet it remains one of the greatest barriers to education for millions of girls.

Starting as a small group of volunteers, we realized that giving out products wasn't enough. We needed to change mindsets, build infrastructure, and advocate for policy reform.

Today, we serve thousands of girls across the country, but our mission remains as personal as the day we started.
