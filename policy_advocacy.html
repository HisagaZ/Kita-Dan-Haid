Championing Systemic Change

While grassroots action is essential, systemic equality is required for long-term health improvements. Our Policy Advocacy program partners actively with policymakers, local councils, and corporate institutions to influence and write legislation addressing menstrual equity.

Through our direct field experience—such as our outreach missions in Jalan Chow Kit and Shah Alam—we gather the necessary data to demonstrate the urgency of menstrual equity to government bodies. These real-world programs provide the evidence needed to mandate structural changes in schools and eliminate taxation on hygiene essentials.
